Emoji Files Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how MggM Apps handles information when you use Emoji Files.

Who we are

Emoji Files is published by MggM Apps. For privacy questions, contact mggmapps@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves progress on your device. This can include your coin balance, solved puzzles, hints, badges, streaks, game history, and settings. We do not operate an account system or our own cloud save service for this information.

You can reset your game progress in the app’s Settings. Your device operating system may also back up app data according to the backup settings on your device.

Rewarded ads

Emoji Files offers optional rewarded ads. If you choose to watch one, Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your device’s IP address, advertising or device identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information. Google may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The game does not receive your advertising profile.

Google’s SDK handles this information under Google’s Privacy Policy and its Google Mobile Ads data disclosure .

Ad privacy choices

Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform presents an ad privacy message before ads load. The advertising SDK uses the choices recorded through that flow to determine whether an ad can be requested and what type of ad may be shown.

Children

Emoji Files is a puzzle game intended for a general audience. The game does not ask players to create an account or provide their name, address, or other profile information. Ads are provided by Google, and their collection and use of information are described in Google’s privacy policy and SDK disclosure linked above.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress remains in the app’s local storage until you reset it or clear the app’s data. Information handled by Google for advertising is subject to Google’s retention practices and policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email mggmapps@gmail.com.
